# Moonbase images

Each numbered entry in `#base` has an image of the hardware or scene it describes. All images come from the film or its original artwork in [the film's source project](https://github.com/jadeqwang/moongazing-public-version).

New frame selections were extracted from `review/moongazing_public_v10_1080p.mp4` in that project. Frame times below are exact extraction times; captions on the site display minutes and seconds. New full-frame images are resized to 1600 × 900 and encoded as WebP at quality 86.
